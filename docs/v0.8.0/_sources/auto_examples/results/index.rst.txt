Plotting and saving results
===========================

Plot differential scattering and directional phase functions, then save and
restore unitful results with their calculation settings.

Six examples cover plotting, archives, coherent and diffuse scattering,
phase-density normalization, angular cuts versus full directional solves,
and the distinction between cross sections and box-normalized coefficients.
The calculations include second-, third-, and fourth-order solves.
