Born orders and interference
============================

Compare cumulative Born orders and inspect coherent amplitude interference.
Field-term diagnostics alone do not certify convergence.

Six examples cover order comparisons, term interference, absorption,
contrast sensitivity, wavelength dependence through fifth order, and coupling
between two spheres. Higher orders retain the linearized dielectric contrast
and spherical Green self cell; decreasing corrections do not certify convergence.
