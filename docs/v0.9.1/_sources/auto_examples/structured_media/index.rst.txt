Structured media
================

Build spheres, finite layers, and combined structured and random samples.
Explore how geometry and relative positions affect directional scattering.
Create concentric core–shell spheres and oriented ellipsoids, or repeat a
geometric motif in a centred finite lattice and inspect coherent interference.
Use finite dielectric cylinders as rod or antenna-like scatterers, with explicit
axis and orientation controls.

Oblique illumination examples specify unit propagation and complex transverse
polarization vectors, comparing linear, circular, and elliptical states on
one anisotropic particle through third Born order.
