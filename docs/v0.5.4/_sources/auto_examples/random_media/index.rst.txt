    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Compare two covariance models with the same refractive index standard deviation and the same length parameter. Their definitions differ: Gaussian covariance is sigma_n2  exp(-r2 / (2  ell2)), while exponential covariance is sigma_n2 * exp(-r / ell). Equal parameters therefore do not give identical real-space profiles or scattering spectra. Here, r is the distance between two points in the medium. The covariance describes how their refractive-index fluctuations vary together as that distance increases.">
