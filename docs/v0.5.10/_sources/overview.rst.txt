Overview
========

BornSim models vector Born-series scattering from finite three-dimensional
dielectric samples. Construct continuous random fields, composite structures,
particle geometries, or finite periodic arrays, then voxelize them on an
explicit grid and compute directional scattering.

The solver retains a linearized dielectric contrast at every Born order.
Finite-sample coefficients and ensemble uncertainty do not by themselves
establish convergence or eliminate discretization and finite-size error.

Start with :doc:`getting_started`, browse runnable :doc:`examples`, or consult
the :doc:`api` and :doc:`theory` for configuration and numerical conventions.
